05 · YOUR QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions

Learn more about Face Reality Acne Bootcamp and preparing for your consultation.

What is Face Reality Acne Bootcamp?

It is a structured acne-focused skincare approach combining professional guidance, products, and follow-up rather than a one-time facial. Your consultation helps determine how the approach may fit your individual skincare needs.

Do I need to stop my current skincare routine?

Not necessarily. Bring your current product list to your consultation so your aesthetician can help assess what to keep, change, or discuss with your medical provider. Avoid making significant changes without appropriate guidance, particularly if you use prescribed skincare or acne medication.
