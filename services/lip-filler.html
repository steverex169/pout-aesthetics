Your visit, explained

What to expect

What to expect from your appointment

Your appointment starts with a consultation and a treatment plan. If lip filler is appropriate for you, your injector will discuss the product, placement, expected swelling, potential bruising, and aftercare before treatment.

Lips can look swollen at first, so the immediate appearance isn't the final result. Swelling and bruising vary from person to person, and your injector will explain what to expect during recovery.

Your injector will also review relevant medical history, contraindications, potential complications, and whether treatment should be postponed or avoided.

Your questions

Frequently asked questions

Have questions about lip filler in Sicklerville, NJ? Here are a few things to discuss with your injector.

Have another question? Contact us

How much lip filler do I need?

It depends on your anatomy and goals. A half syringe may be considered for subtle enhancement, while some plans are built gradually over multiple appointments. Your injector will recommend an appropriate amount after assessing your lips and discussing your preferences.

How long does lip filler take to settle?

Swelling and bruising vary. Many patients assess their healed appearance after roughly two weeks, but your injector will guide you based on your treatment and individual recovery.

Will my lip filler look natural?

Results depend on your anatomy, the product and amount used, placement, and healing. A personalized treatment plan can help align your results with your preferences, but no specific appearance can be guaranteed.

Can I start with a smaller amount of lip filler?

Depending on your anatomy and treatment goals, your injector may discuss a conservative approach or a staged plan. The appropriate amount will be determined during your consultation.

What should I know before getting lip filler?

Ask about the product, expected results, downtime, aftercare, and possible complications. Dermal fillers carry risks, including rare but serious vascular complications. Seek immediate medical attention for unusual or severe pain, blanching or dusky skin, or changes in vision after treatment.
