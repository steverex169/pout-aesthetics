Questions Worth Asking

Before considering an injectable fat-reduction procedure, make sure you understand the product, its intended use, and the risks involved.

Which Injectable Will Be Used?

Ask for the exact product name and its active ingredients. Do not rely on a general treatment label alone.

Is It Approved for the Proposed Area?

Approval and safety depend on the specific product and intended treatment area. Do not assume a product is approved for use throughout the body.

What Are the Risks and Alternatives?

Discuss possible complications, expected recovery, realistic outcomes, and other options before making a treatment decision.

Frequently Asked Questions

Liquid Lipo Consultation FAQs

Learn why the specific injectable and intended treatment area matter when evaluating an injectable fat-reduction service.

Is liquid lipo the same as surgical liposuction?

No. Injectable treatments and surgical liposuction are different procedures with different methods, risks, and considerations. They should not be represented as equivalent.

Can liquid lipo be used anywhere on the body?

No such assumption should be made. Approval and safety depend on the exact product and treatment area. A qualified provider must confirm the intended use and explain whether it is appropriate. Do not assume that approval for one area extends to other parts of the body.

What should I ask during the consultation?

Ask for the product name and ingredients, the intended treatment area, applicable FDA approval status, possible complications, expected recovery, alternatives, and the provider's qualifications for the proposed procedure.

Does a consultation mean I will receive treatment?

No. The purpose of a consultation is to review your goals, medical history, the proposed product and treatment area, and the potential risks and alternatives. The provider can explain whether proceeding is appropriate.
