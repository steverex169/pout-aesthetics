01 · UNDERSTANDING YOUR OPTIONS

What is filler dissolving?

Certain hyaluronic acid fillers can sometimes be broken down using an enzyme called hyaluronidase. Not all filler types can be dissolved this way, and an assessment is needed before treatment.

Our first priority is understanding your history and identifying the safest next step. If you know the filler brand or product, when it was placed, and where it was injected, sharing those details can help inform your consultation.

IMPORTANT SAFETY INFORMATION

Some symptoms need urgent medical attention.

Sudden severe pain, skin-color changes, or vision symptoms after filler require urgent medical attention rather than a routine booking. Vision changes or severe symptoms may be an emergency. Seek emergency care immediately; do not wait for a consultation or a reply to a message.

05 · YOUR QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions

Find out more about filler dissolving and what to discuss during your consultation.

Can all fillers be dissolved?

No. Hyaluronidase acts on certain hyaluronic acid fillers, not all injectable products. The product used and your treatment history need to be assessed before a decision is made.

Can I refill the area right away?

Timing depends on your situation and the provider’s assessment. We’ll discuss whether and when additional filler should be considered based on your individual needs.
