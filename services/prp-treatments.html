Understanding the Treatment

What is a PRP treatment?

PRP preparation involves separating platelet-rich components from a blood sample. It may be used in certain aesthetic procedures, sometimes alongside microneedling.

The technique, evidence, regulatory considerations, and expected results depend on the specific use. The first step is to understand exactly what your appointment includes and what the treatment is intended to address.

During your consultation, our team can clarify the available service, explain its intended approach, and help you understand whether it aligns with your needs and expectations.

Frequently Asked Questions

Questions about PRP

Every PRP service is different. Use your consultation to clarify the procedure, expected recovery, and whether it fits your goals.

Is PRP the same as microneedling?

No. PRP is a blood-derived preparation, while microneedling is a separate procedure. They may be discussed together if the combination is offered and considered appropriate for you.

How many PRP treatments will I need?

There is no universal treatment schedule. The procedure being considered, your goals, and your provider's assessment will help determine whether a series of treatments is appropriate.

Can PRP improve every skin concern?

No treatment guarantees improvement for every concern. Potential outcomes depend on the specific procedure, individual factors, and the evidence supporting that particular use. Your provider can discuss realistic expectations and limitations.

What should I ask before booking?

Ask which PRP service is available, how it is performed, who provides it, what risks and downtime to expect, and what aftercare is required. Share relevant medical history before proceeding.
