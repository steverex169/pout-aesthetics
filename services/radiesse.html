01   /   THE DIFFERENCE

What makes Radiesse different?

Understanding the product's composition and intended use helps make treatment planning more informed.

01

Calcium-based composition

Radiesse contains calcium hydroxylapatite microspheres suspended in a gel carrier. Its composition differs from that of hyaluronic acid dermal fillers.

02

Biostimulatory properties

Radiesse is known for its biostimulatory properties. Your injector can explain the role these properties may play in the proposed treatment and what outcomes are reasonable to discuss.

03

Individualized treatment

Product formulation, treatment technique, and intended area matter. Your provider will assess whether the specific approach is appropriate for you.

Approved uses depend on the specific product and treatment indication. Not every treatment area, dilution, or technique is FDA-approved. Your injector can clarify the intended use and relevant risks during consultation.

04   /   GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions.

Considering Radiesse in Sicklerville, NJ? These answers can help you prepare for a conversation with your injector.

Is Radiesse the same as regular filler?

Radiesse is a type of injectable filler, but its composition differs from common hyaluronic acid fillers. It contains calcium hydroxylapatite microspheres in a gel carrier. Your injector can explain how this affects treatment selection, expected results, and risks.

Can Radiesse be dissolved like hyaluronic acid filler?

Radiesse cannot be reversed with the same enzyme commonly used to dissolve hyaluronic acid filler. This makes careful assessment, informed consent, product selection, and treatment planning particularly important.

Can Radiesse be used on the face, neck, or chest?

Suitability depends on the specific product, treatment area, technique, and intended use. Different areas and formulations do not necessarily share the same approved indications. Ask your injector to explain the evidence, applicable labeling, and risks for your proposed treatment.

What happens during a Radiesse consultation?

Your injector will discuss your goals, anatomy, relevant medical history, and treatment suitability. You can also review expected results, alternatives, possible complications, aftercare, and any questions you have before deciding whether to proceed.
