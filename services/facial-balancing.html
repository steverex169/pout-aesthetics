THE POUT APPROACH

Balance That Starts with Your Features

Facial balancing takes a step back from any one feature and looks at how your face works as a whole. At POUT Aesthetics in Sicklerville, NJ, we use an individualized consultation to understand your proportions, preferences, and what changes—if any—may help create the balance you want.

A WHOLE-FACE APPROACH

What Is Facial Balancing?

Rather than treating a single area in isolation, facial balancing considers the relationship between features such as the cheeks, chin, jawline, and lips. Injectable fillers may be one option, but not every patient needs treatment in multiple areas. The most appropriate plan starts with your goals and anatomy.

Look at the Whole Picture

Consider how the cheeks, chin, jawline, and lips relate to one another instead of focusing on just one feature.

Keep Your Goals Personal

Your preferences and facial anatomy guide the discussion about what you would like to refine.

Choose Only Appropriate Treatments

Fillers may be considered when suitable, but treatment in multiple areas is not a requirement.

GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently Asked Questions

Learn more about facial balancing and what to discuss with your injector before choosing a treatment.

Does facial balancing mean getting filler everywhere?

No. It refers to an overall planning approach, not a required number of syringes or treatment areas. Your provider will discuss which options, if any, are appropriate for your anatomy and goals.

Can facial balancing look natural?

Our focus is on your individual proportions and preferences. Your injector will discuss realistic results and whether a subtle approach is achievable for you.
