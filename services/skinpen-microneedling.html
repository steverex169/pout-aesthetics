01   /   YOUR CONCERNS

What does SkinPen microneedling address?

Understanding the procedure and its cleared indications is an important part of deciding whether it fits your goals.

01

Facial acne scars

SkinPen’s FDA-cleared indications include improving the appearance of facial acne scars in adults age 22 and older.

02

Certain neck wrinkles

SkinPen also has a cleared indication for certain neck wrinkles in eligible skin types. Your provider can explain whether it applies to you.

03

Skin texture goals

Discuss your skin texture concerns and expectations during your assessment. Your provider can explain what the treatment may reasonably address.

Treatment suitability depends on your skin, medical history, and the specific indication. Results vary from person to person.

04   /   GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions.

Have questions about SkinPen microneedling in Sicklerville, NJ? Start here, then speak with your provider about your individual circumstances.

Is SkinPen the same as a facial?

No. SkinPen is a microneedling procedure that creates controlled microchannels in the skin. A traditional facial uses a different approach.

Can I get SkinPen with active acne?

Not everyone is an appropriate candidate. Tell your provider about active breakouts, infections, medications, and your skin history so they can evaluate your suitability and advise whether treatment should be postponed.

What should I expect after treatment?

Redness and sensitivity may occur after treatment. Your provider will explain expected recovery, individualized aftercare, and when to seek advice about symptoms that concern you.

Will I need more than one session?

The recommended treatment plan depends on your concerns, skin assessment, and response to treatment. Ask your provider whether a series of sessions may be appropriate. Results vary.
