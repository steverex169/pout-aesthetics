01 · THE TREATMENT

What does a chemical peel do?

Chemical peels use carefully selected exfoliating solutions to remove or shed layers of surface skin, depending on the formula and depth. They may be considered for concerns such as uneven-looking tone, rough texture, or dullness.

Your aesthetician will explain which peel options are available at POUT and how they fit your skin. The appropriate treatment depends on your skin's condition, sensitivities, current skincare routine, and individual goals.

05 · YOUR QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions

Get familiar with chemical peels before your consultation at POUT Aesthetics.

Will my skin peel afterward?

Visible peeling varies with the product and depth of treatment. Some peels cause little visible flaking, while others may involve more noticeable peeling or sensitivity. Your provider will explain what to expect from the option being considered.

Can I get a chemical peel if I have sensitive skin?

Possibly, but not every peel is suitable. Tell your provider about your sensitivities, current skincare products, medications, and skin history. A consultation helps determine the right option or whether another service is better.
