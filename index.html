What we do

Our Services

Everything here starts with a consult. We talk through what you want to change, what's realistic, and what it involves — then you decide.

01
Lip Filler

Volume, shape and balance. The lip gets mapped and the plan agreed before the first injection — half syringe, full syringe, or a slow build over visits.

45 min · settles in 2 weeks
02
Lip Flip

A few units of tox along the upper lip to roll it outward. Subtle, no filler involved, and a common first step.

15 min · results in 3–7 days
03
Tox

Botox and equivalents for the forehead, elevens and crow's feet. Dosed to soften movement while your expression still reads as yours.

30 min · results in 3–14 days
04
Dermal Filler

Cheeks, chin, jawline and under-eye hollows. Placed conservatively and built across more than one visit rather than all at once.

45–60 min · results immediate
05
Facial Balancing

A full-face plan rather than one area at a time — mapped at consult, then treated in the order that makes sense.

60 min consult · staged treatment
06
IV Therapy

Hydration and vitamin drips given on site by the clinical team.

30–45 min · same day

Kristen Delagol

Nurse Injector

If you've spent time in my chair, you already know — we're probably going to talk about dogs, travel, where to get the best coffee, or what restaurant you need to try next.

Before aesthetics I spent nearly 20 years as an ER nurse. It taught me to stay calm, pay attention to the little things, and never rush people. Those are the same qualities I bring into every appointment today.

I got into aesthetics for one simple reason: I genuinely love it. There's something incredibly rewarding about helping someone catch their reflection and smile because they still look like themselves — just a little more rested, refreshed, or confident.

My hope is that you leave feeling like you found your injector, not just someone who injects.
