Who treats you

Meet the Team

Marla Liberatore

Master Nurse Aesthetic Injector - Owner

@injector_nursemarla

Meet Marla, Founder of Pout Aesthetics Marla Liberatore, RN, is the founder of Pout Aesthetics, but her story in medicine began long before Pout ever had a name. Marla started her nursing career at the Children’s Hospital of Philadelphia, where she developed the compassion, attention to detail, and patient-first approach that would become the foundation of her career. From there, she transitioned into reproductive medicine and infertility, helping patients through some of the most emotional and deeply personal moments of their lives. But Marla always had another dream: plastic surgery and aesthetics. She eventually took the leap into plastics, where she had the opportunity to train and work alongside one of New Jersey’s most highly sought-after plastic surgeons. It was there that she fell in love with the artistry of aesthetics—the ability to combine medicine, anatomy, creativity, and an eye for detail to help someone feel like the best version of themselves. Then came Pout. Pout Aesthetics didn’t begin with a huge team, investors, or a perfect business plan. It began with Marla, a wish, a dream, and the belief that she could create something different. She started Pout on her own and built it patient by patient, never losing sight of what she wanted it to feel like: welcoming, personal, fun, and elevated—while still delivering results rooted in education, safety, and anatomy. Today, Pout has grown into far more than she imagined in those early days, but Marla remains deeply involved in the practice and continues to treat her patients with the same personal approach that helped build it. Outside of Pout, Marla’s greatest accomplishment is her family. She is the proud mom of four children—three boys and one daughter—and, of course, Tank, her English Bulldog, who absolutely believes he deserves equal billing. For Marla, Pout is more than an aesthetics practice. It’s proof that sometimes the things you build from nothing but a wish, a little courage, and a really big dream can become more than you ever imagined.

Marissa is Pout's Office Manager, Marla's right hand, and the behind-the-scenes magic keeping all things Pout running smoothly. With a background in cosmetology and makeup, beauty has always been her thing—and making sure every client has an experience that feels personal, elevated, and a little extra is what she does best. From the little details to the big picture, Marissa's goal is to make every Pout experience feel seamless, special, and anything but ordinary-because everyone deserves to leave feeling confident, cared for, and absolutely amazing. When she's off Pout duty, Marissa is all about life with her husband, their two boys, and their dog, Peroni-equal parts boy mom chaos, family time, and a little glam whenever she can get it.

Cancellation & Rescheduling Policy

To ensure we can provide the best possible service and appointment availability to all of our clients, please review our cancellation and rescheduling policy below.

Appointment Deposit

All appointments and consultations require a non-refundable $100 deposit at the time of booking.

If you choose to proceed with services, your $100 deposit will be applied toward your remaining service balance.

Rescheduling

We understand that plans can change. If you need to reschedule your appointment, please notify us at least 48 business hours prior to your scheduled appointment.

We allow one complimentary reschedule when proper notice is provided.

Rescheduling with less than 48 business hours' notice will result in a $50 rescheduling fee, which will be added to your service charges. Your original deposit remains non-refundable.

Cancellations

All deposits are non-refundable. If you choose to cancel your appointment rather than reschedule, your $100 deposit will not be refunded.

Policy Agreement

By booking an appointment or consultation, you acknowledge and agree to the terms of this Cancellation & Rescheduling Policy.
