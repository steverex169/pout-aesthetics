Frequently Asked Questions

Facial FAQs

Find answers to common questions about choosing a facial and planning skincare around your needs.

How do I choose the right facial?

Start by sharing your skin concerns and goals. Your aesthetician can discuss your routine and recommend the best available appointment type for your needs.

Can I book a facial if I have acne-prone skin?

Often, but the right approach depends on your skin, active irritation, and any medications or skincare products you use. Tell your provider about these factors before treatment. For a more structured acne plan, ask about the Acne Bootcamp consultation.

Can I ask about dermaplaning or chemical peels?

Yes. Ask your provider whether dermaplaning, a chemical peel, or another targeted service is available and appropriate for your skin. Suitability and preparation requirements vary.

Should I change my skincare routine before a facial?

Do not make major changes without guidance. Share your current products and any active ingredients with your provider, who can advise you about any preparation instructions for the selected appointment.
