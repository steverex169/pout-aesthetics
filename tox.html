Wrinkle Treatment, Tailored to You

What Can Tox Treat?

Neuromodulators temporarily relax specific muscles that contribute to expression lines. Common treatment conversations include forehead lines, the “11s” between the brows, and crow’s feet. Your injector will review your facial movement, individual concerns, and the appropriate product and placement for the areas being considered.

Botox vs. Dysport vs. Daxxify

These are different prescription neuromodulators. They have different formulations, dosing, and labeled uses; one is not automatically the best choice for everyone. At your appointment, we can discuss which option may be appropriate based on your goals, medical history, and prior treatments.

What to Expect

We begin with an assessment and explain the proposed treatment, expected timing, possible side effects, and any aftercare. Results generally develop over days rather than immediately, and duration varies by product and person.

Good to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions about wrinkle treatments? Here are a few things to know before scheduling your consultation.

Will Botox make me look frozen?

The treatment plan and dose matter. We discuss the amount of movement you want to maintain and build the approach around your expressions and anatomy. Individual results vary, and no specific result can be guaranteed.

How often do I need tox?

Maintenance is individualized. Your injector will help you decide whether and when another treatment is appropriate based on your response, goals, and the product used.

How do I choose between Botox, Dysport, and Daxxify?

Each is a different prescription neuromodulator, and suitability depends on factors such as your treatment goals, medical history, and prior experience. Your injector can explain the options during your consultation.

When will I see results?

Results are not immediate and generally develop over several days. The timing and duration can vary depending on the product, treatment area, and individual response. Your injector can explain what to expect for your plan.
