Understand your options

Two different approaches

Lip Flip vs. Lip Filler

Both treatments can change the appearance of your lips, but they work differently. The right option depends on your anatomy, preferences, and treatment goals.

Subtle upper-lip change

The Lip Flip

A lip flip uses a neuromodulator to temporarily relax targeted muscles around the upper lip. It may allow more of the upper lip to show when you smile. It does not add volume or physically fill the lips.

This approach may suit people looking for a subtle change in upper-lip appearance rather than fuller lips.

Volume and definition

Lip Filler

Lip filler uses a different type of injectable, commonly a hyaluronic acid dermal filler, to add volume, enhance definition, or provide structure according to your individual goals.

Your injector can discuss whether filler is appropriate if you're looking for a more noticeable change in lip fullness or shape.

Your visit, explained

What to expect

A personalized plan for your lip flip

A lip flip appointment is usually brief. Before treatment, your injector will review your smile, lip movement, expectations, relevant medical history, and whether the procedure is appropriate for you.

Results develop over several days rather than appearing immediately. The degree and duration of change vary between individuals, and the treatment is temporary.

A lip flip can also temporarily affect how your mouth moves. Some people may notice changes when speaking, drinking, using a straw, or performing other lip movements. Your injector will discuss these risks and any relevant alternatives before treatment.
